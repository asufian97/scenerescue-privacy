Privacy Policy

Scene Rescue · Stillwave Studio · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Scene Rescue is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, hints) and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or use Settings → Reset progress.

Internet and third parties

The current version of the game does not connect to the internet and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services. If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released. It will name the advertising partner and describe the data it may collect (such as a device advertising ID), along with your choices.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not request any other permissions.

Children

Scene Rescue does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.
